📝 How to Apply the Logo to Your App

  1. Download your preferred logo design by clicking the button above
  2. Convert the SVG to PNG (1024x1024px) using:
    • Online: svgtopng.com or cloudconvert.com
    • Design tools: Figma, Adobe Illustrator, or Inkscape
  3. Save two versions:
    • icon.png - Full logo (1024x1024px)
    • icon_foreground.png - Logo with transparent background (1024x1024px)
  4. Place both files in the assets/ folder of your project
  5. Run the following commands in your terminal: 
    flutter pub get
flutter pub run flutter_launcher_icons
  6. Rebuild your app and the new icon will appear!
